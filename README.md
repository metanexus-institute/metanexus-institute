# Metanexus Institute

**An experiment in new ways of being human.**

Metanexus Institute is a nonprofit that brings science, philosophy, spiritual practice, art, and technology into the same work. A question can take the form of a story you can enter, a tool you can change, or a practice you can try with other people.

We start from the idea that becoming human is something we do together. Our relationships, tools, and institutions shape what we can notice, imagine, and do. We want more people to have a hand in shaping those conditions. We take play seriously because it lets us revise the rules as we discover what we can do together.

## Metanexus on GitHub

We use GitHub to develop experiments in public. Read the work in progress, tell us where it takes hold or falls short, and try a direction we haven’t considered. Open an issue, propose an edit, or fork a project and see what it becomes in your hands.

Our first project here, **[Incipient Institute](https://github.com/metanexus-institute/incipient-institute)**, is a work of horror fiction you explore through the website of a research foundation. It asks what happens when an institution puts the value of change above the people who have to live with it.
